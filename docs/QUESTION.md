# Interview Question

Your agent will pose this question; the sample answers are responses to it.

> "A REST endpoint in a .NET backend has become slow under load — it was fine
> in testing but times out when real traffic hits it. Walk me through how you'd
> investigate and fix it. Cover where async/await and EF Core fit in."

Your system should transcribe the spoken answer, retrieve the relevant criteria
from the corpus, and produce a structured evaluation: which points the answer
covered, which it missed, and a 1–5 score with a one-line justification.
